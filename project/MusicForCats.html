Music for Cats

I like both cats and music, so I wanted to include a page for cats too. Apparently, cats have their own taste in music. A study from UW–Madison found that cats responded more positively to music made for them than to the classical music they tested. The music used rhythms based on purring and nursing, with pitches suited to cats. Here's the study.

The videos below are mostly birds, squirrels, and nature sounds. I thought it would be fun to include some things for your cat to watch while you look through the songs on this website.
